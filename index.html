What this site covers

Cyber insurance spans carrier and MGA structures, admitted and surplus lines, coverage components with sublimits and exclusions that matter more than the headline policy limit, and underwriting requirements that shift as the market moves through soft and hard cycles. Buyers who understand this structure negotiate better terms than buyers who treat the purchase as a formality.

Every section of this site is organized around a real decision point, who should be in the room, how to compare quotes, what to prove to an underwriter, how to use renewal as leverage, not around generic insurance terminology.

About this site

Cyber Insurance Central is written and maintained by Jay Gitomer, an independent writer and analyst covering cybersecurity vendor risk and market dynamics for over two decades. It is one publication under Machines & Words, an independent writing and analysis practice with no ownership stake, advisory relationship, or paid arrangement with any insurance carrier, broker, or MGA covered on this site.

Coverage here starts from security posture, not policy language. Carrier requirements, underwriting expectations, and renewal terms are analyzed on their own merits, informed by ongoing tracking of vendor stability and security market shifts elsewhere in the author's work.

No sponsored placements. No affiliate arrangements with carriers or brokers. If that changes for any page on this site, it will be disclosed on that page directly.
